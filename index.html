Privacy Policy

Sereńko — Christian Hymn Book App · Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sereńko app ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. We built this app to be simple and respectful of your privacy — it does not require an account, does not run ads, and does not collect or sell personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not:

  • Require you to sign up, log in, or provide any personal details
  • Collect your name, email address, or contact list
  • Track your location
  • Use analytics or advertising SDKs
  • Sell or share any data with third parties

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To make the App work, a small amount of data is saved locally on your device only, using Android's standard app storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else. This includes:

  • Your favourite songs
  • Your recently opened songs
  • App display preferences — dark mode, color theme, font size, and search keyboard mode

Uninstalling the App removes all of this data from your device. We have no way to access, view, or recover it.

3. Permissions the App Uses

The App requests a limited set of Android permissions, used only as described below:

  • Internet access — used only to check for and download app updates from our public GitHub release repository, and to open external donation links (UPI apps, PayPal) and your email app if you choose to contact the developer.
  • Install packages — used only so you can install an app update you've chosen to download. The App will never install anything without your explicit tap to confirm.
  • Storage — used only to save a downloaded update file before installing it.

4. Donations

The App's donation screen opens your own UPI app, PayPal, or email app to let you choose to support development. The App itself does not process, store, or have access to any payment or financial information — that happens entirely within the third-party app you choose to use.

5. Hymn and Song Content

Hymn lyrics and categories included in the App are bundled with the App itself and loaded locally — they are not fetched from or sent to any server, and are not linked to you in any way.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from any user.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
